Section 3: Mathematical Formulation & Scoring Engine (फॉर्मूला और लॉजिक)

Total: 3 Questions
Q9 Performance Score calculate karne ka exact mathematical formula kya hai? Weights kaise decide kiye?
Examiner Kya Check Kar Raha Hai: Core domain logic, weighted sum formulation, and grading policy understanding.

Aapka Ideal Jawab (Hinglish):

"Sir, Performance Score ek 100-point normalized index hai jo continuous evaluation rubrics par based hai:

Performance Score = (Final_Marks × 0.35) + (Attendance × 0.15) + (Study_Score × 0.10) + (Assignment_Score × 0.10) + (Quiz_Average × 0.10) + (Practical_Score × 0.10) + (Internal_Marks × 0.10)

• Yahan Study_Score = min((Daily_Study_Hours / 6) × 100, 100) normalize kiya gaya hai (6 hours = 100%).
• Weights ki Justification:
- Final Marks (35%): Sabse bada summative indicator hai.
- Attendance (15%): Regularity aur classroom discipline ko reflect karta hai.
- Continuous Formative Components (50%): Assignments (10%), Quizzes (10%), Practicals (10%), Internals (10%), aur Study Hours (10%) milkar student ke day-to-day engagement ko measure karte hain."

Technical Keywords: Weighted Linear Combination Normalization to 100-Point Scale Summative vs Formative Rubrics
Q10 Academic Risk Level (High, Medium, Low) kaise determine kiya jata hai?
Examiner Kya Check Kar Raha Hai: Multi-criteria classification logic aur risk threshold boundaries.

Aapka Ideal Jawab (Hinglish):

"Sir, Risk sirf ek single test ke marks par decide nahi hota, balki humne Multi-Factor Risk Scoring Rule Engine banaya hai:
• Agar Performance Score < 50 → +3 Risk Points.
• Agar Attendance < 75% → +3 Risk Points (Institutional mandate violation).
• Agar Practical Score < 60% → +2 Risk Points (Hands-on deficiency).
• Agar Internal Marks < 60% → +2 Risk Points.
• Agar Study Hours < 2 hrs/day → +2 Risk Points.

Final Classification:
- Risk Points ≥ 5: High Priority / High Risk (Immediate Teacher Intervention zaroori hai).
- Risk Points 2 to 4: Medium Risk (Mentoring & Quiz Monitoring).
- Risk Points 0 to 1: Low Risk (Academic Stability)."

Technical Keywords: Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis Risk Thresholding Additive Penalty Scoring
Q11 Student Portal ka 'What-If Simulator' mathematical point of view se kaise kaam karta hai?
Examiner Kya Check Kar Raha Hai: Sensitivity analysis aur dynamic simulation logic.

Aapka Ideal Jawab (Hinglish):

"Sir, What-If Simulator ek Prescriptive Sensitivity Analysis Tool hai.

ΔScore = Simulated_Score − Current_Baseline_Score
Isse student ko visual confirmation milti hai ki e.g. 'Agar mai attendance 72% se 85% kar lu, toh mera score +2.0 points badhkar Medium Risk se Good category me aa jayega'. Ye student ko behavioral change ke liye motivate karta hai."

Technical Keywords: Sensitivity Analysis Behavioral Nudge Delta Score Evaluation

Section 4: Machine Learning Integration (मशीन लर्निंग सवाल - सबसे महत्वपूर्ण)

Total: 5 Questions
Q12 Aapke project me already mathematical formula tha, toh Machine Learning model add karne ki kya zaroorat padi?
Examiner Kya Check Kar Raha Hai: WHY ML? Rule-based system vs Machine Learning ka critical distinction.

Aapka Ideal Jawab (Hinglish):

"Sir, ye bohot important question hai!
• Rule-based formula deterministic aur rigid hota hai. Wo fixed linear weights assume karta hai, jabki real students me variables ke beech me non-linear interactions hote hain (e.g., kisi student ke study hours kam hain par practical bohot strong hai, toh uska final outcome linear sum se alag ho sakta hai).
• Machine Learning Model historical academic data ke patterns se learn karta hai. Ye unseen students par generalized probabilistic prediction deta hai (e.g., student ke High Priority me jaane ki probability 72% hai).
Isliye hamare project me dono hain: Formula provides transparent baseline, aur Machine Learning provides predictive foresight!"

Technical Keywords: Deterministic vs Probabilistic Non-Linear Feature Interaction Generalization to Unseen Data
Q13 Aapne kaunsa Machine Learning Algorithm use kiya aur dusre algorithms (jaise Linear Regression ya SVM) ke mukable kyu choose kiya?
Examiner Kya Check Kar Raha Hai: Algorithm selection reasoning and trade-off analysis.

Aapka Ideal Jawab (Hinglish):

"Sir, humne Random Forest (Ensemble Learning) use kiya hai — RandomForestRegressor for score prediction aur RandomForestClassifier for Risk Priority.
Kyu Choose Kiya:
1. Overfitting Resistance: Single Decision Tree jaldi overfit hota hai, lekin Random Forest multiple trees ka ensemble (bagging) use karta hai jisse variance reduce ho jata hai.
2. Non-Linear Mapping: Linear Regression multi-dimensional tabular data ke complex interactions capture nahi kar pata, jabki Random Forest non-linear decision boundaries easily create karta hai.
3. Feature Importance: Random Forest natively Gini Impurity ya Variance Reduction ke through har feature ka exact contribution percentage calculate karke deta hai.
4. Robust to Outliers: Student marks data me occasional outliers hote hain, jisme Random Forest SVM ya Neural Networks se zyada stable rehta hai."

Technical Keywords: Random Forest Ensemble Bagging & Variance Reduction Gini Impurity / Feature Importance
Q14 Aapka Train/Test Split kya tha aur Model ke Evaluation Metrics kya aaye?
Examiner Kya Check Kar Raha Hai: Quantitative verification, validation rigour, and exact statistical metrics.

Aapka Ideal Jawab (Hinglish):

"Sir, humne 500 records dataset par 80:20 Train-Test split kiya tha (400 training instances, 100 test instances) with random_state=42 for reproducibility.

1. Regressor Metrics (Performance Score):
• R² Score (Coefficient of Determination): 0.62 (62% variance explain ho raha hai).
• Mean Absolute Error (MAE): 4.29 marks (average error sirf 4 marks hai 100 me se).
• Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE): 5.43 marks.

2. Classifier Metrics (Risk Category Classification):
• Overall Accuracy: 91.0%.
• Precision & Recall: High Priority class ke liye Precision 0.89 aur Recall 0.86 aayi hai."
